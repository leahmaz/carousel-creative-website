What’s inside

Three steps, from zero to a complete picture of your customer.

Most ad creative underperforms because it’s built on assumptions, not evidence. Voice, Insight, and Persona build a detailed understanding of your customer entirely from what real people actually said—the exact process we use at Carousel Creative on every client strategy.

  1. V

    Voice

    Collect real customer language, word for word, from the places your customer actually talks.

  2. I

    Insight

    Pull out what matters: the pain points, the triggers that make people buy, the objections that make them hesitate, and the alternatives they were using before.

  3. P

    Persona

    Deep psychographic profiles that tell you how your customer thinks, what they value, and what they need to hear to convert.
