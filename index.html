The opportunity

Better Meta performance starts with better creative.

Today, the biggest opportunity to improve performance isn’t in targeting, bidding, or campaign settings—it’s in the creative itself.

Good creative helps the algorithm understand who should see your ads, improving your targeting. It brings in higher quality traffic, improving your conversion rates and average order value. And it attracts different types of customers, making your account able to scale more efficiently.

We study customer motivations, identify different messaging opportunities, and continuously test new ideas to understand what resonates with your audience.

The result is lower ad costs, more sales, and happier customers.

One team

Creative performs better when the people making it also run the campaigns.

Many brands separate creative production from campaign management. One team makes the ads and another team runs them. The result is often a disconnect between what’s being created and what’s actually resonating.

Our process is integrated—we don’t separate creative from media buying. Campaign performance informs what we make next, and creative insights influence how campaigns are structured.

The person behind the work

Meet Leah.

I started my career analyzing advertising data at Canada’s largest Facebook advertiser, studying patterns across millions in ad spend to understand what drives performance.

In 2017, I launched Carousel Creative and have been helping brands improve their Meta advertising through a combination of customer research, creative strategy, production, and media buying.

My background is a little unusual for an advertiser. I studied Psychology before moving into advertising, and over the years I’ve become increasingly interested in understanding how people make decisions, what motivates them to buy, and how those insights can be translated into better creative.

I work with a small team of trusted specialists, partnering with brands that want a more thoughtful approach to Meta advertising—one where strategy, creative, and media buying work together to continuously improve performance.

I’ve shared my approach through presentations and workshops for Founders Institute, the Canadian Public Relations Society, McGill University, Canadore College, and as a speaker at Ad World.
